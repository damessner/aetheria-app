Product Requirement & Technical Specification: "Aetheria: The Cognitive Quest"1. Executive Summary & Evidence-Based Clinical Foundation1.1 Core Value Proposition"Aetheria" transforms evidence-based Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) and Behavioral Activation (BA) into an immersive fantasy world-restoration mobile application. Designed to maximize clinical efficacy while maintaining deep user engagement, Aetheria translates proven psychological techniques into a 6-week gamified campaign.1.2 Grounding in Clinical LiteratureThe application architecture directly implements empirical findings from key clinical trials and meta-analyses:Dual-Skill Efficacy (Furukawa et al., 2025): The app prioritizes dual-skill combinations—specifically Behavioral Activation (BA) + Cognitive Restructuring (CR) and BA + Problem Solving (PS) / BA + Behavior Therapy for Insomnia (BI). Research shows that combining two core CBT skills yields superior, durable symptom reduction ($\Delta PHQ-9 = -0.67$) up to 26 weeks compared to single-skill interventions or control conditions.Optimal Intervention Duration (Bae et al., 2023): Interventions structured within a 4- to 6-week window demonstrate significantly higher effect sizes ($SMD = 0.77$) than longer programs ($SMD = 0.43$), avoiding attrition while ensuring full content exposure.Notification Strategy (Bae et al., 2023): Intrusive in-app push alerts are removed in favor of asynchronous weekly email digests and scheduled check-in summaries, preventing alert fatigue and shame loops.Incentive & Gamification Loops (Bae et al., 2023; Shahsavar & Choudhury, 2025): Structured reward systems and context-engagement mechanics (video playback, gamified challenges) drive significantly stronger treatment adherence ($SMD = 0.72$).Target Audience Severity (Arean et al., 2016): Active CBT and cognitive control tools demonstrate their strongest differential impact on users with moderate depression ($PHQ-9 \ge 10$).Circadian & Night-Owl Adaptations (Furukawa et al., 2025): Behavior Therapy for Insomnia (BI) mechanics are specifically adapted for shift-workers and late-night programmers, replacing strict rigid wake-up rules with adaptive stimulus control and blue-light UI transitions.2. 2.1 Currency & Economy BalanceVitality Points (VP): Earned by executing real-world Behavioral Activation (BA) tasks. VP is spent to repair damaged sanctuary zones, plant cosmetic flora, and upgrade realm lighting.Clarity Shards (XP): Earned through psychoeducational micro-lessons and successful thought-reframing battles. Used to level up avatar stats (Mind Shield, Logic Edge, Focus Pulse).Rest Shields: Automatically granted when logging off or taking rest days. Rest Shields shield the user's progress bar from resetting, guaranteeing a "Depression-Safe" environment.2.2 Anti-Frustration & Depression-Safe MechanicsCumulative Consistency Bars: Replaces rigid single-day streak resets. Missed days pause progress and accumulate "Rest XP" that grants double rewards upon return.Task Micro-Decomposition ("Break Down" Button): Automatically decomposes overwhelming tasks into atomic sub-steps (e.g., "Write API endpoint" $\rightarrow$ "Open IDE" $\rightarrow$ "Define route signature" $\rightarrow$ "Write test case").Late-Night / Night-Owl Mode: Detects active sessions between 00:00 and 06:00, automatically transitioning the UI to a low-blue-light dark mode and replacing high-energy physical quests with somatic breathing or stimulus-control micro-quests.3. Card Battler Algorithm & Combat MechanicsThe thought-reframing battle uses a deterministic combat calculation modified by the accuracy of distortion identification:$$\text{Total Damage} = \text{Base Damage} \times \text{Distortion Multiplier} + \text{Evidence Bonus} + \text{Reframe Score}$$4.1 Combat Multiplier LogicDistortion Identification Phase:Correct Distortion Selected (e.g., Identifying "Catastrophizing"): $\text{Distortion Multiplier} = 1.5$Incorrect Distortion Selected: $\text{Distortion Multiplier} = 0.8$Evidence Validation Phase:User selects 1–2 validated facts from their pre-filled "Evidence Vault": $\text{Evidence Bonus} = +10 \text{ HP Damage}$Reframing Generation Phase:User inputs a custom alternative thought or selects a structured card:$$\text{Reframe Score} = \min\left(20, \; \text{Length Penalty} \times \text{Keyword Match}\right)$$5. 6.1 Recommended Tech StackClient Framework: Flutter or React Native (Ensures multiplatform support for iOS/Android from a single codebase).Game Rendering: Rive Engine or Lottie for vector-based 60fps interactive animations without Unity engine overhead.Local Storage (Local-First): SQLite (via WatermelonDB / Drift) encrypted with SQLCipher (AES-256). All reflection journals remain local to the device.Backend Framework: Node.js (TypeScript) with PostgreSQL hosted on HIPAA/GDPR-compliant infrastructure.Email Digest Service: SendGrid or Amazon SES executing weekly scheduled cron jobs for asynchronous progress summaries (avoiding push notifications).6.2 Data Security & E2EE ArchitectureZero Analytics Trackers: Complete removal of third-party advertising SDKs (no Facebook Pixel, Google AdMob, or AppsFlyer).End-to-End Encryption (E2EE): Personal thoughts, journal entries, and crisis responses encrypted using keypairs generated via the device's Secure Enclave / Keystore.7.
